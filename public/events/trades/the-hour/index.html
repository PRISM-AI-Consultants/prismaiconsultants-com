The hour

Three jobs, built live

No slides about the future of AI. We take real problems from the people on the call and build the fix on screen, so you see exactly how it works in a shop like yours.

Job 01

The call you missed

You are on a roof, under a sink, or driving. The phone rings and the customer calls the next name on the list. We show an AI line that answers in your shop's name, takes the details and gets the job on your calendar.

You leave with: what an answering line should say for your trade, and what it should hand off to you.

Job 02

The estimate that went quiet

You quoted it, they said they would think about it, and nobody followed up. We build a follow up that goes out on a schedule, in your voice, without you remembering to send it.

You leave with: a follow up sequence you can use on your open estimates this week.

Job 03

Paperwork after dark

Quotes, job notes and invoices written up at night. We turn a voice note from the truck into a clean quote or write up, so the evening is yours again.

You leave with: a way to talk your notes instead of typing them.
