Two kinds of shop. Different fixes.

Most AI pitches to the trades are about getting more leads. For a lot of owners that is the wrong fix. We start by figuring out which one you are.

"I have more work than I can handle."

You need capacity

Calls go to voicemail, quotes go out late, invoices pile up. More leads on top of that just means more dropped balls. The fix is getting time back: answering, quoting and following up without you doing all of it.

"I could take on more jobs."

You need follow up
