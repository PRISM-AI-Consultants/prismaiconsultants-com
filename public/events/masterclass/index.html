The idea under all five

Capture. Connect. Direct.

Most people get junk out of AI because they put almost nothing in. The whole hour hangs on three words.

Capture

Give it the information

One sentence in gets one generic answer out. Give AI the whole picture and it stops guessing.

Connect

Stop retyping

Hook AI up to your email, calendar, drive and customer list, so the context flows in without you copying and pasting.

Direct

You steer it

You are the director. Start with the goal, and when AI drifts, you bring it back.

Parts of the hour run live with people from the audience. Live means things can go sideways, and that is on purpose: you watch us steer AI back, which is the real skill.
