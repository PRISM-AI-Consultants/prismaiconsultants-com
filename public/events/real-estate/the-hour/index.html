Forty seconds on a real listing

On the September 22 episode of AI Hustle with Dr. Jeff, we pulled a real Allentown commercial listing and had an agent build the pre-meeting brief live. The listing reports $72,600 in gross income and $66,600 net operating income. That means only $6,000 in total expenses. But taxes alone are about $5,649, and insurance is another $3,400. Two line items already exceed the stated total, before repairs, vacancy, or utilities. That gap is the leverage.
