What it does

Front end: it answers

  • Answers every call, 24 hours a day, in English or Spanish
  • Finds out who is calling, what they need and how urgent it is
  • Sends every caller to your team or CRM with a callback task, so no lead sits
  • Texts the caller your booking link, only when they say yes, and never late at night
  • Gets graded after every call and learns from its mistakes

Back end: it calls for you

  • Calls businesses for quotes, prices and availability
  • Handles errands like checking a reservation or a vendor
  • Reports back what it learned, with the full transcript
  • Works in Spanish too. Its first real call was to a hotel in Panama

How we run it
  • It always says it is an AI if anyone asks, and it never pretends to be a person.
  • When a call is recorded, it says so before recording starts.
  • It only states facts you gave it. It never invents a price, a time or a promise.
  • It never takes card numbers or payments over the phone.
  • It texts only people who said yes, and only between 8am and 8pm Eastern.
  • It does not cold call. AI calls to people's phones need their consent first, and Pennsylvania's updated telemarketing law (SB 992), effective October 19, 2026, requires written consent for automated and AI-voice sales calls. We set yours up to follow it.
